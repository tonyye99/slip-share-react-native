# SlipShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

SlipShare is an app for splitting restaurant bills with friends. This policy explains what the SlipShare mobile app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** Your email address, and your name if you give one. If you sign in with Apple, Google or GitHub, that service shares your name and email address with us, and Google and GitHub also share a link to your profile picture. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only get Apple's private relay address.
- **Your receipts.** The shop name, items, prices, tax, service charge and totals you save, including the text the AI read from the photo.
- **Your bill splits.** The names you type for the people you split a receipt with, who had each item, who paid the bill, who has paid back, and what each person owes. Friends' names are only used to show the split to you, and to friends you send a link to.
- **Friend links.** If you send a friend link for a receipt, the names friends type when they open it and the items they tick.
- **Scan counts.** How many receipts you scanned each day, so we can apply the daily scan limit.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, advertising ID, or usage analytics, and the app has no ads or trackers.

## Receipt photos and AI

SlipShare uses OpenAI to read receipt photos. Before your first scan, the app asks for your permission and does not send any photo until you agree.

When you tap **Read receipt**:

1. The app shrinks the photo and sends it, through our server, to OpenAI.
2. OpenAI reads the shop name, items and prices and sends that text back.
3. You check and edit the result, and only what you save is stored in SlipShare.

**SlipShare does not store your receipt photos.** OpenAI does not use photos sent through its API to train its models. OpenAI may keep them for up to 30 days to watch for abuse and then deletes them. See [OpenAI's API data policy](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/your-data) for details.

You can turn off receipt scanning at any time in **Account > Privacy**. The app will ask again before your next scan. Receipts you already saved are not affected.

## Who can see your information

- **Only you can see your receipts and splits in SlipShare**, unless you send a friend link for a receipt. When you tap **Send totals**, your phone's share sheet opens and you choose where the text goes, for example LINE or WhatsApp. That app's privacy policy covers what happens next.
- **People with a friend link.** A friend link opens that one receipt on the SlipShare website. Anyone who has the link can see the shop name, items, prices, the names on the receipt (your line shows your display name), who had each item and who paid, and can add their name and tick items. They don't need an account. The link only works while you have it turned on; turning it off stops it for everyone, and sharing again makes a new link.
- **Service providers we use to run SlipShare:**
  - [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) hosts our database, sign-in and server code.
  - [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/) reads receipt photos, as described above.
  - Apple, Google and GitHub handle sign-in if you choose them.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

## How we protect it

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). Our database only lets you read your own data. Friend links use a long random code that can't be guessed, and they only show the one receipt they were made for.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and receipts until you delete your account.

To delete your account, open **Account** in the app and tap **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account, every receipt you scanned with the names and payments on it, your scan counts and your permission for receipt scanning. Your SlipShare account also works on the SlipShare website, so this deletes it there too.

## If a friend sent you a link

You don't need an account. When you type your name and tick items, they're saved as part of your friend's receipt, and everyone with the link can see them. Your browser keeps a random key, so only your browser can change your picks. Your friend can see and change everything on their receipt, and it's deleted when they delete their account. To have your name removed, ask your friend or contact us.

## Your choices

- See and edit your receipts in the app.
- Turn a receipt's friend link on and off on that receipt.
- Turn receipt scanning off and on in **Account > Privacy**.
- Delete your account and all your data in **Account**.
- Ask us a question about your data using the contact details below.

## Children

SlipShare is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how receipt photos are handled, the app will ask for your permission again before your next scan.
